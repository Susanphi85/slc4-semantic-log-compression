SLC4
Semantic Log Packer

Experimental lossless semantic compressor for structured JSON logs. It fingerprints schemas, columnizes values, chooses generic codecs per field, and uses Zstandard as the final entropy/compression backend. CSV and SQL dumps are read natively and come back byte-identical. Everything runs in your browser — your logs are never uploaded.
